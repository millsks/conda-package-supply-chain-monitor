No --ok appears on this screen

No green, no tick, no "all clear", no celebratory copy. The only hues present are --unk slate, --err burnt orange and --accent. An empty queue that renders green is CPM-SM-C1 arriving through the back door.

CPM-SM-C1CPM-FR-6

The freshness bar still renders

Zero rows does not suspend provenance. The reader still learns when the rollup was computed, where the evidence cut-off fell, and which policy run produced the emptiness they are looking at.

CPM-AD-11CPM-FR-37

The counter-line is in the same block

Not a footnote, not a second panel below the fold, not a link. It shares the .emptyblock with the emptiness statement, separated by one hairline, so the two cannot be read apart.

E2CPM-SM-C1

Each counter row carries its own evidence

Source, observation time and a qualifier, on the .ev footline — the same grammar the rest of the product uses. A counter-line without provenance is just an assertion.

CPM-FR-37

The 3px rule is --unk, not --accent

The block concerns unresolved and unmapped state, so the provenance rule is coloured to match its content. Never colour the rule with a hue that contradicts the block.

Three emptinesses, three screens

E2 has a queue and nothing in it. E3 has an inventory and a filter that excluded it. E4 has no derived state at all. Conflating any two of them is a bug.

E2E3E4

A killed worker still reads running

The state the reader needs to see rather than a gap. The heartbeat column carries the amber footline that makes the staleness legible; the state chip itself is untouched, because running is what the ledger recorded.

CPM-APP-S06

The freshness bar carries em dashes, not zeros

Cells with no value take E5's em dash in mono-value. A zero or a fabricated timestamp here would be a claim, and there is nothing to claim.

CPM-AD-11E5

Run-ledger states are the second closed enumeration — running, succeeded, failed, idle. They are markerless and never take an outcome hue.

pyarrow-legacy is not in the monitored inventory

The monitor watches a curated list of packages. This identifier is not on it, so nothing has been collected about it and no status exists for it — neither a conclusion nor an absence.

How the inventory changes

The inventory is a curated watchlist versioned in the repository and changed by pull request. Adding a package is a review, not a setting: open a pull request against the watchlist and the next policy run picks it up.

The string not_found does not appear on this page

Not in the heading, not in the chip, not in the body, not in the footline. It is a member of OutcomeState with a specific meaning, and reusing it here would tell the reader a collector looked. None did.

Middleware, not a per-view concern

One rule covers every HTMX interaction in the product: facet swaps, evidence disclosure, queue transitions and job polling. A per-view check would be forgotten on the fifth view someone adds.

HTMX boundary

Empty body, never a partial

HX-Redirect plus zero bytes. Returning a fragment — even a correct one — leaves htmx free to swap something into the target.

HTMX boundary

The sign-in page states the consequence

"Nothing you submitted was written", the return URL, and the item's unchanged state. A reviewer who was mid-transition needs to know whether to redo it.
